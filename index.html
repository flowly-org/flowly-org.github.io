Your calendar, as background

Google Calendar events sit above the board without becoming tasks. Flowly flags the few that need preparation (tickets, travel, a trip away from home) so nothing sneaks up on you.

Columns and priorities are tags you define: add Review or Waiting, reorder them, choose what counts as done. Unfinished work carries over.

Your day in your pocket

On the phone, Flowly keeps only what you need on the go.

  • Today, at a glance. Your tasks from every workspace, with what's carried over and what's urgent.
  • One tap to track. A big ▶ on every task starts the timer; the bar at the top stops it.
  • Swipe to review. Right is done, left stays to do. Clear the day in thirty seconds, with undo.
  • Photo of a list. Snap the to-do list on your desk and add it in one go.

AI that knows your day

Bring your assistant to your tasks, or let Flowly's come to you.

  • The concierge (Pro) on Telegram and in the app: a morning brief that also checks your calendar (“the concert is Thursday: tickets?”), urgent alerts, and tasks added, moved or timed by chat.
  • Send it a photo of a list and the tasks are there, in the workspace you name in the caption.
  • Delegate to Flo (Pro Plus): hand over research, drafts and plans. Flo checks what it can do, works on it and reports on the task, sources included. It never sends, books or buys anything.

  • Workspace-level MCP for organization flows and automations, with service tokens that act as a bot member.

Built for teams, comfortable alone

  • Telegram groups and Slack (Enterprise). The team's plan every morning, live updates, questions answered in the chat, whiteboard photos turned into tasks.
  • Safe in a group. Private tasks never appear; people who aren't in the workspace can only read; everyone else acts as themselves.
  • Roles & invitations. Owners, admins and members. Invite by email or share a join link.
  • Notifications for assignments, urgent tasks and comments: in the app, on your desktop and on Telegram.
  • Your colours. Each workspace tints Flowly while you're in it. Light, dark or follow the system, in six languages.

Works where your work already is

Connect a whole workspace or just one project.

  • GitHub Issues become tasks; closing either side closes the other; comments sync both ways.
  • Google Calendar Several accounts, read-only; events as background, preparation flagged.
  • Email in Forward to team@in.flowly.net-style aliases and it becomes a task.
  • Slack Updates in a channel, or the full Slack app with @Flowly and /flowly.
  • Telegram Your personal concierge, and the team bot in your group.
  • Webhooks Signed outgoing events and an incoming URL that creates or updates tasks.
  • Google Drive & OneDrive Attach documents straight from your drives.
  • Your own AI (Enterprise) An OpenRouter key or an on-premises OpenAI-compatible model, with data kept exclusive to it.

Track time, invoice it, get paid

  • A ▶ on every card, in the phone app and in your desktop tray. Or log it afterwards: 1:30, 45m, 2h.
  • Billing is off until you switch it on per workspace. Each project uses your rate, its own rate, or opts out.
  • Invoices from unbilled time, with rounding and tax. Print to PDF, export CSV, or download the e-invoice: FatturaPA for Italy, XRechnung for Germany, Factur-X for France, checked against the official validators.
  • Pay online: connect your Stripe account and every invoice gets a card-payment link; it's marked paid when your client pays.
