Works where your work already is

Connect a whole workspace or just one project.

  • GitHub Issues become tasks; closing either side closes the other; comments sync both ways.
  • Slack Completed, assigned and urgent tasks posted to a channel.
  • Webhooks Signed outgoing events and an incoming URL that creates or updates tasks.
  • Email Forward an email to a workspace address and it becomes a task.
  • Google Drive & OneDrive Attach documents straight from your drives.
  • Telegram Your concierge, alerts and morning brief in chat.

AI that knows your day

Bring your assistant to your tasks, or let Flowly's come to you.

  • ChatGPT, Claude and any MCP client. Connect Flowly as a connector and ask “what's left today?”, add tasks, log time or search past work, with your permissions.
  • Workspace-level MCP for organization flows and automations, with service tokens that act as a bot member.
  • The concierge (Pro): a personal assistant on Telegram and in the app. Morning brief, urgent alerts, and it adds or moves tasks for you, in your language.
  • Bring your own model. Workspaces can use their own OpenRouter key or a private OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and keep their data exclusive to it.
